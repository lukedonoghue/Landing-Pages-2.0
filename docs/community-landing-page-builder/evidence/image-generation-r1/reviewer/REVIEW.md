## Prioritized findings

### P1 - Hero illustration disclosure is invisible at mobile and tablet widths

The hero image is realistic enough to be read as a Clarentis workspace, but the sighted-user caption `Illustrative workspace image` is hidden behind the image at 390 x 844 and 768 x 1024. The caption is present in the DOM and placed over the photo, yet the `<picture>` paints on top of it. At 390px, the caption occupies approximately x=204-378, y=692-713; `elementFromPoint` at its centre returns the hero image. At 768px, it occupies x=582-756, y=738-759 and is similarly covered. Desktop at 1024px shows the caption correctly. The 320px placement is also covered when scrolled into view.

Source: [`index.html:47`](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/image-generation-r1/project/index.html:47) classifies the image as illustrative; [`index.html:62`](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/image-generation-r1/project/index.html:62) renders the visual disclosure; [`styles.css:57`](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/image-generation-r1/project/styles.css:57) positions it; [`styles.css:159`](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/image-generation-r1/project/styles.css:159) gives the mobile photo a stacking position above it. Make the caption visibly paint above the photo, then recheck its contrast and crop at 320, 390, and 768px.
