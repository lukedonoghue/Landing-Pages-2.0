Common questions

Know what you're asking for.

The initial conversation helps Clarentis understand the work before quoting. These answers cover the main starting points.

Does the £60 monthly starting price apply to everyone?

No. The published £60 - £150 monthly range is indicative for sole trader or micro-business support. The final fee depends on the work, transaction volume and complexity.

Can Clarentis help before my company is set up?

Yes. Company formation support, Companies House registration guidance and first-step tax and accounting setup are among the listed services.

Do I have to sign up for monthly support?

The fee guide also lists VAT return preparation, Self-Assessment filing and one-off MTD setup. Ask about the work you need in the free consultation; the proposal will define the scope.

What should I include in my first email?

Tell Clarentis whether you are a startup, sole trader or limited company, which services you need, and any upcoming deadline you want to discuss. You can explain the details in the consultation.
