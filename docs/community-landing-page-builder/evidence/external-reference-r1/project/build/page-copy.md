## Customer feedback and FAQ

Quote: "I'm really pleased with our new office, the finish and the whole process was excellent." - David Pert, Clitheroe. Source: The Garden Room Co. About Us.

Heading: The practical questions.

Q: Will I need planning permission? A: Many outbuildings may fall within permitted development rules, but the answer depends on the property, location, size, and use. Discuss your plans with the team and check the relevant local requirements.

Q: Can I use the room throughout the year? A: The team uses insulated SIP construction and can discuss glazing, heating, and electrical options for your intended use.

Q: Do I need a foundation? A: Yes. The right foundation depends on ground conditions, levels, access, drainage, and the room size. The team will assess what suits your site.
