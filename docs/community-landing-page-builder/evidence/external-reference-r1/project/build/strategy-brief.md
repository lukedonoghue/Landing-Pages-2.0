# Strategy brief

Research date: 2026-09-20. Sources are public pages of The Garden Room Co. and the user-supplied Green Retreats reference.

## Buyer and action

- Buyer: a UK homeowner considering a garden room for work, leisure, or a home business. Likely comparing designs, practical fit, and builders before requesting a quote.
- Traffic intent (inferred, no keyword data supplied): bespoke garden rooms and garden offices, with North Wales and the listed service areas as likely local intent.
- Primary offer: an initial free design consultation and no-obligation quote. Primary CTA: **Enquire online**.
- Source conversion contract: the [home page](https://gardenroomco.com/) invites visitors to enquire online about a free design consultation and quote. The [contact page](https://gardenroomco.com/contact-us/) uses a form with required name and email, optional phone, a source selector, and optional marketing consent. A team response is promised; no instant price or booked time slot is promised. The public Contact Form 7 endpoint is not reused.
- Local journey: same enquiry type. The page collects name and email, with optional phone, postcode, intended use, message, and separate optional marketing consent. A local receiver confirms synthetic preview submissions only. The production endpoint remains unconfigured.
- Decision obstacle: uncertainty about what can fit, planning requirements, materials, and the effort of managing a build.
- Trust anchor: first-party built-project photography and named customer feedback, supported by the firm's stated 20+ years of experience and managed project process.

## Brand and presentation

- Verified formal brand guide: none found.
- Official production page typography: browser-computed H1 and section heading use **Reem Kufi**; meaningful body paragraphs use **Poppins**. The extracted first-viewport report initially caught entrance animation before the hero text appeared, so a second 2.5-second browser check verified the actual heading and prose stacks. Both are locally loaded from licensed Google Fonts files.
- Source palette: official transparent logo has white lettering and green `#A4BD15`; live header uses charcoal about `#3C3C3B`.
- Applied palette: `#2E3430` charcoal for framing and readable dark areas; `#FFFFFF` and cool `#F4F6F3` for open reading surfaces; logo green `#A4BD15` for primary action and fine accents; deeper `#304B41` for supporting contrast. Green is restrained because the first-party photography already carries natural colour.
- Visual direction: **considered** (large, inspectable project photography), **practical** (clear process and short answers), **personal** (bespoke fit and named projects). The roofline and fine green rule echo the official mark. No legacy decorative background, animation-dependent text, or boxed brochure grid is carried over.
- Green Retreats reference role: image-led presentation, varied media rhythm, visible next step, buyer questions, and project inspiration. Its ranges, pricing, proof, brand styling, wording, and imagery do not transfer.
- Image rights: public first-party photographs and logo are used for a local preview. Ownership or permission to republish should be confirmed by The Garden Room Co. before launch.
- Optional modules: no brochure, tracking, CRM, or publishing. Local preview receiver exists solely for form QA and does not deliver leads.
