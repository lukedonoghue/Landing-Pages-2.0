# The Garden Room Co. landing page strategy

Research date: 2026-09-20. Public pages only. The supplied Green Retreats page is a presentation and completeness reference, not a source of claims for this business.

## Buyer and action

- Buyer: a homeowner or home business owner considering a garden office, studio, gym or flexible extra room. The likely search intent is garden rooms, bespoke garden offices and garden studios. No campaign keyword data was supplied.
- Decision stage: comparing design fit, build quality, practical constraints and what happens before paying.
- Desired action: request the free design consultation and no-obligation quote through an enquiry form.
- Primary objection: uncertainty about whether the space, foundations, planning position, specification and total quote will suit a particular garden.
- Credible answer: an initial conversation leads to a no-obligation site survey and design consultation. The team then makes computer visualisations before the buyer commits financially. The official site says an in-house team handles the project with a single point of contact.
- Trust anchor: named, photographed projects on the official portfolio, 20+ years of team experience as stated on the home page, and first-party customer feedback on the About page.

## Source conversion contract

The official home page pairs "Book your free design consultation and quote" with "ENQUIRE ONLINE" linking to its contact form. The contact form requires name, email and message; phone and discovery source are optional. It includes an optional marketing checkbox. The implied destination is a business enquiry, with staff follow-up. There is no public booking calendar. The local page uses one exact CTA label, "Request a free design consultation", and a popup enquiry form. The form is an endpoint-free local preview. It never claims to send a lead. Production delivery is not configured or tested.

## Brand and design

- Verified brand guide: none found.
- Official-site rendered typography: the production H1 "Stunning Garden Rooms" paints in Reem Kufi Regular, with computed stack `Reem Kufi, Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, sans-serif`; the meaningful project paragraph paints in Poppins Regular, computed stack `Poppins, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`. Measured at 390 and 1440 pixels in `brand.json`.
- Applied typography: the same families, bundled from Google Fonts with their OFL licences. Reem Kufi is used for display headings, Poppins for copy and controls. The new scale and layout are adjusted for readability; the type families are not substituted.
- Source palette: logo white plus yellow-green; rendered site accents near `#a4bd15`, charcoal near `#3c3c3b`.
- Applied palette: deep evergreen `#18332d` grounds the header and final contact section; charcoal `#26332d` carries body text; white and pale cool grey keep project photography clear; brand yellow-green `#a4bd15` is reserved for primary action and fine highlights. Warm timber in real photographs gives a natural secondary color without a flat all-green page.
- Visual adjectives: architectural (strong building photography and measured alignment), approachable (clear first-person process language), crafted (real materials and close project captions).
- Composition: photographic full-bleed hero with unframed copy, narrow proof band, editorial use section, varied real-project gallery, an unobscured interior project photograph, material/specification split, process sequence, a single large customer quote, concise practical questions and a firm final action. Motion is limited to hover feedback; reduced-motion setting removes transitions.
- Visual-age checkpoint: retain logo, source colors, actual projects and actual font families. Use quieter palette proportions, larger image scale and more open spacing than the official site's older template styling. Green Retreats informs the confident photo-led rhythm and complete decision journey, without adopting its range names, prices, endorsements or branded assets.
