    Who We Are

    We are a team of experienced professionals with 20 years of expertise in designing, manufacturing, and delivering top-of-the-line garden office rooms. Utilising the most advanced materials and production methods, we create warm, efficient, secure and functional spaces that will boost your productivity at home.

    Our company takes pride in delivering a seamless experience for our clients. With a dedicated project manager, we handle every aspect of the project from start to finish, eliminating the need for you to coordinate multiple contractors.

    Our goal is to make the process as stress-free and enjoyable as possible for you. Once you decide which garden office is best for you, all you have to do is sit back and look forward to exceptional service and quality. We guarantee you will love your finished building for years to come.

    Unbeatable Quality

    From our structurally insulated panels to our eco-friendly EDPM roofs, you can remain confident that we are conscious about our choice of suppliers to ensure the work we do is as ecologically sound as possible.

    Bespoke

    With the increased availability of flexible working now more than ever is a great opportunity to utilise the space, you have to create a bespoke work area which you can use to maximise productivity when working from home.

    Swap that arduous commute to the office for a casual saunter through your own garden; bringing a new meaning to the phrase “I’m in the office today”.

    Our Process

    We understand that your garden room needs to be as unique as you are. That’s why we offer a simple and bespoke process that allows us to design and supply a garden room that meets your specific needs and complements your outdoor space.

    We have a range of basic options to get you started. From there, we’ll work with you to customize the design to your exact requirements, ensuring a quality build every step of the way.

    The possibilities are endless and are only restricted by your imagination and the space you have to work with. We’ll guide you through the process step-by-step, ensuring that your garden room is everything you envision and more.

    Say Hello!

    Get in touch to start this process and take advantage of our expertise in this area. We offer a free no-obligation initial consultation which leads to a free no-obligation, site survey and design consultation. From there we will create detailed computer models with visualisations of how the garden office will look in the space you have. Then and only then will you need to commit to the project financially.
