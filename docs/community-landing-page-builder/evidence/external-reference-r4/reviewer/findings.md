# Independent visual and product review

**Result: blocked as a local final.** Review date: 2026-09-20. This was an independent, read-only review of the run's `skill/SKILL.md`, `project/index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, source strategy/copy/image notes, and `http://127.0.0.1:4176/`. Public comparison sources: [The Garden Room Co.](https://gardenroomco.com/), its [contact](https://gardenroomco.com/contact-us/), [About](https://gardenroomco.com/about-us/) and [FAQ](https://gardenroomco.com/faq/) pages, and the supplied [Green Retreats reference](https://www.greenretreats.co.uk/). No page or skill edits were made.

## Confirmed blockers

1. **The desktop/tablet hero obscures the proof-bearing room.** At 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440 pixels, the heading and lead run across the building's glazed opening and visible interior. The heavy left scrim makes those details harder to inspect. This conflicts with the skill's explicit rule to protect glazing, finished surfaces and other essential subject detail in hero photography. The 390-pixel layout solves this by separating photo and copy. At 1280 x 600, the following proof band starts at y=593, leaving only 7 pixels of it in view and no readable cue to continue. Evidence: [768 hero](./768x1024-first.png), [1024 hero](./1024x800-first.png), [1280 short-height hero](./1280x600-first.png), [1440 hero](./1440x900-first.png), [390 hero](./390x844-first.png).

2. **The quote answer omits a published commercial offer.** The live [contact page](https://gardenroomco.com/contact-us/) visibly advertises a like-for-like price promise for a lower comparable quote. The landing page's quote section gives factors that affect scope but omits that promise, and the strategy does not explain its exclusion. This is directly relevant to the reference's price-comparison job and to a buyer deciding whether to enquire. Evidence: [mobile quote section](./390x844-quote-section.png).

3. **Service-area fit remains unanswered.** The [business site](https://gardenroomco.com/) lists North Wales, Chester, London, Manchester, Merseyside and Shropshire location pages. The landing page gives only the St Asaph address in its footer; neither the main copy nor the enquiry entry tells a visitor whether their location is in scope. The skill names service area as a considered-purchase question, while the strategy records the locations but has no coverage answer. Evidence: [final action](./390x844-final-section.png), [footer](./390x844-site-footer.png).

4. **Available customer proof is reduced to one narrow quote.** The client's [About page](https://gardenroomco.com/about-us/) publishes seven customer accounts covering workmanship, communication, deadlines and management. The final page uses one excerpt about design and budget; its process and build-quality sections have no nearby customer corroboration. The supplied reference has a broader proof and media rhythm, and the skill blocks unexplained removal of relevant first-party evidence. The strategy calls this a justified difference without explaining why the available client reviews were excluded. Evidence: [process](./390x844-process-section.png), [single testimonial](./390x844-testimonial-section.png), [reference composition](./reference-1440x900-full.png).

## Observations

- The page looks custom and current rather than like a legacy brochure. Its real [project gallery](./390x844-project-grid.png) and [interior feature](./1440x900-inside-section.png) remain readable and unobstructed at inspected sizes. The logo is visible immediately, the source yellow-green is used sparingly, and the applied Reem Kufi/Poppins stacks match the live business site's computed heading/body families. No formal brand guide was found in the source strategy; there is no typography substitution to justify.
- The form-first action preserves the source consultation/quote intent. Header, hero, middle and final CTAs opened the same dialog at 390 and 1440 pixels without changing page position or URL, and closing restored focus. The verified public number `07803 362187` is legible near the top and at final contact. The dialog's [mobile](./390x844-modal.png) and [short-height](./1280x600-modal.png) submit actions are visible. Empty-submit [mobile](./390x844-validation.png) and [desktop](./1440x900-validation.png) states show a summary and inline errors; the summary link focuses the named field. Keyboard tabbing reached the [privacy link](./390x844-privacy-focus.png) visibly above the action row.
- The customer-facing process opener says "The official process starts...", which reads like an internal research note. The client's [FAQ](https://gardenroomco.com/faq/) also says waste removal is generally the customer's responsibility, a practical scope detail absent from the page. These are copy improvements, not separate confirmed visual failures.

## Coverage and limits

Actual pixels were inspected at **390 x 844, 768 x 1024, 1024 x 800, 1280 x 600, and 1440 x 900**. Each has first-screen, full-page and open-dialog captures in this folder; readable gallery/interior and later-section crops were inspected as well. The [mobile](./390x844-full.png) and [desktop](./1440x900-full.png) full-page captures show composition; the linked section crops above support text-level judgments. The reference was viewed in Chrome at mobile and desktop after dismissing its cookie overlay, with [desktop](./reference-1440x900-clear.png) and [mobile](./reference-390x844-clear.png) first screens and full-page captures saved here.

All local image files decoded, no page/network errors appeared, and document width equalled viewport width in the five captured states. This does not clear the product blockers above. The enquiry is explicitly an endpoint-free **local preview**; no lead was submitted and production delivery was not verified. Detailed form lifecycle, static scripts, accessibility scan and Lighthouse were outside this focused review and were not rerun. No fixes or retests were made because this task was review-only.
