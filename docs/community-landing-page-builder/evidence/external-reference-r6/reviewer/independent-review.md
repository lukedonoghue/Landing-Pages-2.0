# Independent visual and editorial acceptance review

Reviewed 2026-09-21. **Verdict: hold full editorial acceptance.** The local page is visually coherent and readable in the inspected captures, and its principal quote offer, brand type, contact number, and material claims track the client's public site. Two self-service buyer journeys present on the client/reference sites are missing or too thin to call the page complete. This is an independent review of the final artifacts, not a form/backend or publication verdict.

## Findings

### 1. Material: buyers cannot compare the client's room options before enquiring

The design section promises a room tailored to the plot, but its choice guidance is four labels (size/layout, glazing, cladding, power) without concrete examples or a way to inspect the client's styles. The detail section gives three broad considerations, and the price section asks readers to compare full specifications without showing what this company's own common specification or selectable alternatives look like. The three project cards demonstrate uses, but they do not answer how a prospect can choose between configurations. See [index.html:126](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/external-reference-r6/project/index.html:126), [index.html:145](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/external-reference-r6/project/index.html:145), and [index.html:161](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/external-reference-r6/project/index.html:161). Readable evidence: [design/details crop](./desktop-design-details.png) and [price/process crop](./desktop-price-process.png).

The [client's garden-room page](https://gardenroomco.com/garden-rooms/) publicly lists eight styles and specifications; the [reference home page](https://www.greenretreats.co.uk/) gives a prominent range comparison and ways to explore options. Those exact ranges and prices belong to Green Retreats and should not transfer. The missing buyer job is a credible, client-specific way to judge fit and inclusions before a call. A small verified option comparison or a visible route to the client's existing styles/specifications would close it.

### 2. Material: the client's advertised brochure path is absent

The current [client home page](https://gardenroomco.com/) promotes a new garden-room brochure in its top banner, and its navigation includes "View Brochure". The [reference page](https://www.greenretreats.co.uk/) also treats brochure access as a research step. The local page has no brochure mention or link in its main journey or footer: see [index.html:243](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/external-reference-r6/project/index.html:243). Compare the [client first screen](./client-top.png) with the [local first screen](./desktop-top.png). This drops a real lower-commitment offer for visitors who are not ready to request a quote. A quiet secondary route is sufficient; it should point to a verified working client destination. The brochure destination itself was not confirmed because the public `/brochure/` page returned a verification challenge during this review, so this finding concerns omission of the advertised route, not proof of a working downloadable file.

The compact office image fills the width immediately before two customer quotes. At desktop and mobile size it shows a plain, tightly fenced building and unfinished-looking ground, while no caption identifies the project or connects it to either named account. It is a distinct first-party-looking project image, but at this scale it carries less craft or lifestyle evidence than the preceding project photographs and the supplied reference's more varied media. See [index.html:193](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/external-reference-r6/project/index.html:193), [styles.css:122](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/external-reference-r6/project/styles.css:122), [desktop crop](./desktop-reviews.png), and [mobile crop](./mobile-reviews-faq.png). ## Scope and limits

Reviewed only the named project and skill criteria, relevant linked references, public client/reference pages, final HTML/CSS, and existing rendered screenshots. The builder's acceptance conclusions and test-result booleans were not used; its image/claim inventories served only as pointers checked against visible artifacts and public sources. No project or skill files were changed. No new local page captures were made; the report's local-page crops come from the existing screenshots. The public source/reference screenshots were captured for visual comparison.

This was not an interactive form-state suite. No live form submission, account connection, backend check, image-rights determination, deployment, or Cloudflare review was performed. The 320x700 inspection covers the first screen only; full-page lower-section conclusions come from the other five sizes, especially readable 390px and 1440px crops. FAQ-open and menu-open states were not inspected. Public site content may change after the retrieval date.
