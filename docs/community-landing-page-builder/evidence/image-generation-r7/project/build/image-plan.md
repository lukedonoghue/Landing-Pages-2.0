## Exact submitted prompts

Hero: Create one photorealistic editorial photograph for a UK small-business accounting landing page. Wide 16:9 horizontal composition. A calm, modern independent-business worktable in natural morning light: a closed dark-navy laptop, simple calculator, neatly stacked blank invoices and receipts, pen and small plant. The meaningful objects sit on the RIGHT half; the LEFT half is genuinely quiet pale wall and uncluttered desk suitable for separate webpage headline text. Sophisticated but un-staged, tactile paper and realistic shadows, white, muted teal and navy accents. No people, no readable words or numbers, no logo, no charts, no fake financial proof, no watermark. High-resolution photo, not an illustration or composite.

Bookkeeping: Photorealistic editorial photograph for a UK small-business accountant's website, 4:3 landscape. Close natural view of hands organising a modest pile of real-world paper receipts and invoices into labelled-but-unreadable folders beside a laptop, no face visible. The setting feels like an independent business doing its monthly bookkeeping, clean but lived-in, soft daylight, cool navy and muted teal details. Documentation contains no legible words, names, amounts, logos or identifiable businesses. Avoid fake dashboard screens, perfect stock-photo staging, extra fingers, watermark. This is clearly an illustrative generic scene, not evidence of a client.

Tax: One photorealistic editorial photograph for a UK accounting and tax support landing page, 4:3 landscape. An orderly small-business desk in a bright office at the moment of reviewing tax and VAT records: simple paper folders, an open blank notebook, calculator, calendar with no legible dates, a laptop closed or with dark blank screen. A hand with a pen can enter the frame but no face or identifiable person. A different composition from an overhead receipt-sorting photo: camera at desk height, strong geometry, soft side light, restrained navy and sea-glass accents. No readable text or figures, no logos, no fake HMRC documents, no charts, no watermark. Realistic materials and natural photography.

Consultation: One realistic editorial photograph for the 'free initial consultation' section of a UK small-business accounting website, 4:3 landscape. A calm conversation at a small office table between two generic adults, seen from a side/back angle so nobody is posed as a named employee or client. One person gestures toward a simple blank notebook while the other listens; friendly but businesslike, no handshake, no staged smiles at camera. Natural window light, modern understated surroundings, a touch of navy and muted teal. Faces are not prominent. No logo, no legible documents, numbers, screen content or text, no watermark. This scene is illustrative only, not evidence of an actual Clarentis meeting.
