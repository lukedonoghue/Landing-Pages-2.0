# Clarentis landing page strategy

Research date: 2026-09-20. Primary source: https://clarentis.co.uk/ . The supplied site is a single-page accounting, tax and bookkeeping site. No separate media, team, review or privacy page was discoverable in the public navigation. The rendered first viewport contains an official logo and no content photography.

## Buyer and action

- Buyer: a UK startup founder, sole trader or small limited company owner who needs routine accounting support and wants to know scope and cost before committing.
- Likely stage: comparing providers or seeking help with records and deadlines. Search intent is inferred; no campaign keywords were supplied.
- Desired action: email Clarentis to request a fixed-fee quote, with a free initial consultation as the first step.
- Mechanism: Clarentis discusses the business, deadlines and records, reviews scope and transaction volume, then provides a fixed-fee proposal before setup and ongoing support.
- Primary objection: uncertainty about what is included and what it will cost. The source's fee guide and scope review answer this without promising a universal price.
- Trust anchor: a specific published scope, indicative fee guide and four-step onboarding process. No rating, testimonial, credential or named staff member is asserted.
- Geographic fit: the source names UK startups, sole traders and small limited companies. It does not publish a narrower service area or a nationwide service guarantee.

## Source conversion contract

The source's dominant CTA is `Request your fixed-fee quote`, linked to `info@clarentis.co.uk` through email. Other email CTAs offer a free consultation and joining. There is no visible form, consent text, booking calendar or on-site success state. The visitor is asked to describe their business type and support needs. The local page retains an email action using the exact primary CTA label. The adjacent explanation says the email app opens and the visitor must press Send. It does not claim a submission or confirmation. The verified public secondary phone is 07467 474356, shown near the top and in the final contact area. No backend or Cloudflare connection is selected.

## Brand and design

- Verified source colors from the rendered page: navy `#08294a`, teal `#159aaa`, pale blue-gray `#f6f8fb`, pale teal `#eaf7f8`, and white. The downloaded official logo retains its own exact colors.
- Applied colors: navy `#08294a` for headings and footer, darker teal `#0e7180` for accessible filled actions, white and cool off-white for reading surfaces, pale teal for a quiet process area, warm gray `#f4f3ef` for the fee area, and deep terracotta `#974c33` for small editorial accents. The action teal is darkened for white-text contrast; the warm neutral and accent keep the interface from becoming an enlarged navy/teal logo.
- Verified brand-guide typography: none found. Official-site heading `h1` and prose `p.lead` both declare `Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui...`; Chromium rendered `.SF NS` because Inter was not loaded. Evidence: `build/brand.json` and the 390/1440 source captures. Applied typography uses the same declared system stack without a loaded Inter file, preserving the actual official-page glyph choice on this host. This is a deliberate native UI font stack, which may resolve to the platform's system face elsewhere.
- Three adjectives: clear, calm, practical. Moderate headline scale, open white space and readable pricing express clarity; low-contrast surfaces and natural-light imagery express calm; compact service details and direct price qualifications express practicality.
- Shape and rhythm: mostly unframed editorial sections, fine rules, square-to-soft corners (at most 8px), full-width photo with protected copy space, alternating text/image orientation, then a dense fee table and simple process steps. No testimonial cards or fabricated badges.
- Motion: no necessary animation. Native details disclosure and ordinary anchor scrolling work without script.
- Visual-age checkpoint: preserve the real logo, navy/teal identity and Inter intent. Do not carry forward the source's 92px desktop heading, tightly tracked display text, pill-heavy controls or large blank hero that puts the main action at the fold. Hero photo is illustration, never proof.
