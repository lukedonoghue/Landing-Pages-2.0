Exact submitted prompts:

**Hero** (`exec-edc9ecc2-3d92-48c3-ab0d-e1f9d5862fb0.png`): Create a photorealistic editorial photograph for a UK small-business accounting landing page. Wide landscape 16:9 composition. An unoccupied, modern but lived-in small business worktable in natural daylight: a closed paper ledger, a neat short stack of blank-looking receipts, a simple calculator, a fountain pen, and the edge of an open laptop with an intentionally unreadable neutral screen. Keep all useful objects in the RIGHT half, with soft uncluttered off-white wall and desk negative space across the LEFT half for separate webpage text. Subtle navy and teal details, tactile paper and wood, believable British independent-business setting, candid rather than glossy stock photography. No people, no logos, no legible text, no numbers, no charts, no certificates, no fake documents, no added graphic text. Bright clear focus, natural colors, realistic materials.

**Bookkeeping** (`exec-8cf86e52-810f-4a77-8024-da22c6dcc3ac.png`): Photorealistic editorial photograph, landscape 4:3. Close but natural view of small-business bookkeeping in progress on a clean desk: a simple calculator, bank-card terminal, a few unprinted receipt slips, tabbed folders without writing, and a pencil beside a laptop showing only a soft neutral, unreadable interface. No person or company identity. The arrangement should communicate sorting daily transactions and keeping records current. Distinct from a wide empty hero desk: tight composition with objects filling frame, soft side daylight, restrained navy, teal and white accents, natural materials, crisp focus. No logos, no readable text, no figures, no certificates, no charts, no graphic overlays.

**Deadlines** (`exec-4a580e19-c8d9-4109-82bd-ade5decb678d.png`): Photorealistic editorial photograph for a UK small-business accountant landing page, landscape 4:3. A quiet, human-scale office shelf and desk corner set up for tax deadlines: wall calendar with only blank squares and no readable dates or writing, a few tidy unlabelled folders in an open filing tray, one simple analog clock, closed laptop, and daylight from a window. Focus on the organized calendar and files, showing deadline planning rather than a claim of an actual client's results. No people, no logos, no readable text, no financial numbers, no certificates, no charts. Natural materials and restrained navy/teal accents, warm daylight, contemporary but not glossy, composition visually distinct from an overhead bookkeeping desk.

**Consultation** (`exec-58cf1591-3483-4d8c-82e2-0171bcd09bc4.png`): Photorealistic editorial photograph for a UK small-business accounting landing page, landscape 4:3. An illustrative first consultation at a modest bright meeting table, photographed from an oblique angle with only two adults' hands and forearms visible, no faces or identifying features. One hand points to a blank page in an open plain folder while the other holds a pen; a closed laptop and simple unlabelled file sit nearby. Communicate a practical discussion of business needs before a fixed-fee proposal. Natural window light, realistic hands and materials, restrained navy and teal details, candid local-professional mood rather than staged stock. No logos, no readable text or numbers, no charts, no certificates, no visible identity, no graphic overlay.
