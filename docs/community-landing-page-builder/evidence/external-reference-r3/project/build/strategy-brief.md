# Strategy brief

Research date: 2026-09-20. Client: [The Garden Room Co.](https://gardenroomco.com/). Reference: [Green Retreats](https://www.greenretreats.co.uk/).

## Decision

- Buyer: a homeowner considering a custom garden office, studio, gym or flexible room, likely comparing built examples and trying to understand fit, process and cost.
- Traffic intent: inferred searches for bespoke garden rooms and garden offices; no ad or keyword data was supplied.
- Desired action: enquire online about a free design consultation and quote. Exact page CTA: **Enquire online**.
- Delivery mechanism: the company's in-house team discusses requirements, designs a bespoke room with CAD, then manages the build with one point of contact. The [about page](https://gardenroomco.com/about-us/) describes a free initial consultation, followed by a site survey and design consultation before financial commitment.
- Main objection: whether a room can fit the garden and intended use, and what the design and building process involves. The page answers with real project examples, a concrete process and qualified FAQ answers.
- Strongest trust: first-party completed project photographs and project pages; one customer statement from the official [about page](https://gardenroomco.com/about-us/). No aggregate rating, price or promised timeline is used.

## Source conversion contract

The [home page](https://gardenroomco.com/) says "Book your free design consultation and quote" and uses "ENQUIRE ONLINE" to reach the [contact page](https://gardenroomco.com/contact-us/). This is a form journey. The source form requires name, email and message; phone and referral source are optional. Its marketing checkbox is a separate optional opt-in. The source does not show a booking calendar or instant quote. The local page retains the form and offer. Local submission goes only to a disposable preview receiver, with success displayed after its confirmation. No live endpoint is reused.

## Design direction

Three adjectives: **considered, capable, welcoming**. Generous spacing and project-scale photos make the investment feel considered; clear process and concise detail convey capability; warm timber photography and direct copy keep it welcoming.

- Source palette: logo and production page use charcoal around `#3c3c3b`, white, and lime around `#a3bc1a`. The official white/lime logo is used on charcoal only.
- Applied palette: charcoal `#26332d` for headings and header, white `#ffffff` and cool off-white `#f6f7f3` for calm reading, brand lime `#a3bc1a` for the primary action, and muted olive `#657567` for support. The rust tones in the real cedar photography supply a contrasting warm note.
- Verified brand guide: none supplied or found.
- Official-site typography: Chromium measured the visible home H1 "Stunning Garden Rooms" in **Reem Kufi** and a substantial home-page paragraph in **Poppins** at 390px and 1440px; both painted with custom web-font glyphs. Evidence: `build/brand.json` and associated screenshots.
- Applied typography: the same Reem Kufi and Poppins families, bundled from the public font files used by the production page. SIL Open Font License files are in `assets/fonts`. No font substitution is planned.
- Hierarchy: compact navigation, large but restrained hero heading, readable Poppins prose, and useful section headings. Letter spacing stays at zero.
- Shape and spacing: mostly unframed sections, crisp image frames, light rules, generous vertical space, and one actual form panel. No large corner rounding or stacked cards.
- Image rhythm: one wide, unobscured real project photo above an unframed hero-copy band, one project-detail image, a three-project portfolio, then a quiet text-led FAQ and form. The wide hero placement keeps the room's glazing free of text.
- Motion: only modest hover/focus transitions, removed for reduced-motion users.
- Brand motifs: lime linework from the official mark and clean rectilinear frames resembling garden-room elevations. The older centred hero typography and dense navigation of the source are not carried forward.

## Reference adaptation

| Reference beat | Buyer job | Garden Room Co. adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Large garden-room hero | Immediate category recognition | Own completed-project photograph, own logo, bespoke offer and enquiry action |
| Range and use discovery | See what a room can become | Real office, salon and relaxing-room project examples, without invented product ranges or pricing |
| Proof and process rhythm | Reduce uncertainty | Own portfolio links, sourced customer statement, actual consultation/design/build steps |
| Repeated next step | Keep the action available | Same "Enquire online" label leading to one inline form |

Green Retreats' product prices, showrooms, celebrity endorsement, reviews and assets are not client facts. The reference informs pacing and image scale only.

## Scope

Selected: static landing page, local preview receiver for synthetic form QA, acceptance notes. Not selected: brochure, analytics, CRM or publication. The company has a brochure on its site, but the core offer here is a design consultation and quote. First-party website images are included for local preview; publication rights remain for the business to confirm.
