## FAQ

## Questions worth asking before you build

Do I need planning permission? Rules depend on the building and your property. The team can discuss the question with you, but any required permission should be confirmed for your site.

Will it suit my garden? The design is bespoke. The team discusses dimensions, access, ground conditions and intended use before confirming a proposal.

Can I use it throughout the year? The company describes SIP wall and roof construction, plus glazing and heating options. Comfort depends on the final specification and how you use the room.

When do I commit? The company describes an initial consultation, followed by a site survey and design discussion, before financial commitment. Ask the team to explain the steps for your project.
