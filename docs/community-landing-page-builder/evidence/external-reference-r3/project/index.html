Good to know

Questions worth asking before you build

Every garden and intended use is different. Your consultation is where the details become a plan.

Do I need planning permission?

Rules depend on the building and your property. The team can discuss the question with you, but any required permission should be confirmed for your site.

Will a room suit my garden?

The design is bespoke. The team discusses dimensions, access, ground conditions and intended use before confirming a proposal.

Can I use it throughout the year?

The company describes structural insulated wall and roof construction, plus glazing and heating options. Comfort depends on the final specification and how you use the room.

When do I commit?

The company describes an initial consultation, followed by a site survey and design discussion, before financial commitment. Ask the team to explain the steps for your project.
