# The Garden Room Co. landing page strategy

Research date: 2026-09-20. This is a local preview for a UK homeowner considering a garden office, studio, gym, salon or multi-use room. Likely traffic intent is inferred from the official site's garden room and garden office language; no keyword or ad data was supplied.

## Decision

- Desired action: open an enquiry form for the official free design consultation and no-obligation quote.
- Offer/result: a bespoke garden room planned for the buyer's intended use and site.
- Delivery mechanism: the team discusses requirements, offers a no-obligation site survey and design consultation, prepares CAD visualisations, and manages the build with a single point of contact. The exact site and specification determine the quote.
- Primary objection: whether the design will suit the garden, what it will cost, and whether planning, foundations, services and maintenance add complexity.
- Trust anchor: named first-party projects in Walthamstow and Moreton, plus the official 20+ years team-experience and in-house project-management statements.
- Geographic fit: the official site lists North Wales, Chester, London, Manchester, Merseyside and Shropshire. These are examples of published locations, not a promise to serve every postcode. The form invites the visitor to share location for confirmation.
- Public phone: 07803 362187, shown as Alex's number on the official contact page and as the organization telephone in its markup. The page uses 'Call the team' rather than assuming a title or role for Alex.

## Source conversion contract

The homepage says 'Book your free design consultation and quote' and labels its CTA 'ENQUIRE ONLINE'. The contact page uses a form with required name, email and message; phone and discovery source are optional; marketing consent is a separate optional checkbox. The form posts to Contact Form 7 and promises a team response, but no public success wording was verified. The new page uses one exact primary CTA label, 'Enquire online', and one modal form. Its local preview receiver confirms a synthetic request without forwarding or storing a lead. Production delivery remains unconfigured.

The official contact page also publishes a like-for-like price promise. The page mentions it only in the official terms: a lower like-for-like quote for a garden room can be brought to the team, who say they will beat it. Exact eligibility criteria are not published, so visitors are told to confirm the comparison with the team.

## Visual direction

Three adjectives: **considered** through generous spacing and restrained typography; **tangible** through large, named project photography; **approachable** through plain-language explanations and a visible telephone alternative.

- Verified source palette: logo white and a bright yellow-green, approximately `#a9be0f`; source site uses dark charcoal header and white content.
- Applied palette: deep green-charcoal `#202b26` for header and final call to action; near-black `#24302a` for prose; soft cool off-white `#f4f6f2` for quiet section surfaces; the source lime `#a9be0f` only for the primary action and small emphasis. Cedar tones come from the photographs, not a large UI fill.
- Verified brand-guide type: none supplied or discovered.
- Official rendered type: `h1` 'Stunning Garden Rooms' computes to Reem Kufi, 300, on the official homepage; the project-intro paragraph computes to Poppins, 400. Captured in `work/public-source/rendered-brand.json` by Chromium at 390 and 1440 pixels.
- Applied type: locally bundled Reem Kufi for headings and Poppins for prose, matching the official computed families. The source's oversized hero scale is reduced for fit and readability. Bundled files came from the same public font resources loaded by the official page, with OFL files in `assets/fonts/`.
- Shape/surface: square editorial bands, fine rules, small-radius image treatments, no card-grid default.
- Composition: unframed copy and photo in a full-width hero; staggered project images; an image-led case study; dense practical information; an unobstructed modal.
- Image roles: real first-party project photographs for all proof. No generated imagery or review portraits.
- Motion: subtle transitions only, disabled under reduced motion.
- Visual-age checkpoint: retain the official logo, lime accent, photo evidence and type; do not copy the source site's crowded navigation, blueprint texture or centered hero copy over glazing. Green Retreats is the presentation benchmark for image rhythm, breadth of buyer questions and repeated action, not a source of client facts or assets.
