## Questions

- Eyebrow: Good questions to ask early
- H2: What to know before you enquire.
- Intro: Your garden and intended use will determine the right answer. These are the practical points we discuss with customers.
- Will I need planning permission? Garden outbuildings may fall within permitted-development rules when the relevant criteria are met. Requirements depend on your property, location and intended use, so check the proposal before assuming permission is unnecessary.
- What kind of foundation will I need? There is no single best base for every garden. We discuss concrete pads, ground screws, steel frames and plastic lumber systems; the right choice depends on ground conditions, access, drainage, levels and building size.
- Can I choose the exterior finish? Yes. We discuss Western red cedar, treated timber and composite cladding. Each has a different appearance and maintenance profile, so the right finish is part of the design conversation.
- Can the room include electricity or heating? We can discuss electrical connection and certification through our suppliers, along with heating. Tell us how you intend to use the room so those requirements can be included in the proposal.
- Do you work in my area? Our website shows projects and location pages for North Wales, Chester, London, Manchester, Merseyside and Shropshire. Share your town or postcode in your enquiry so we can confirm your address.
