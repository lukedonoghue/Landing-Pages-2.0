    Bespoke Garden Salon

    Moreton

    We had the pleasure of working on this at home garden hairdressing and training salon.

    The building is 7m x 4m with corner glazing and patio sliding doors in Anthracite UPVC.

    The Cladding is Red cedar which the client has stained. The building is SIPS construction with a Rubber Roof. There are both internal and external spotlights and double USB sockets throughout. The building has a water connection and the wastewater is pumped away with an independent system, which ties into the existing wastewater at the property.

    The client has made a saving of £800 PCM by moving into a home-based studio making this a project that will pay for itself in 3 years.

    • Garden Room Type: Garden Room Extension
    • Dimensions: 7m x 4m
    • Year Built: 2019
    • Location: Moreton
