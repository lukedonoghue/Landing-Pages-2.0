## Actionable findings

### P2 - The process ends before the build it promises

[index.html:52](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/external-reference-r5/project/index.html:52) promises a managed build from start to finish, and the process eyebrow at [index.html:152](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/external-reference-r5/project/index.html:152) says "From first idea to finished room." Yet all four steps at [index.html:157](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/external-reference-r5/project/index.html:157) stop at reviewing the quote. Buyers get no answer to what happens after commitment, who coordinates installation, or how timing is set. The [client's About page](https://gardenroomco.com/about-us/) supports a dedicated project manager and start-to-finish handling; its [FAQ](https://gardenroomco.com/faq/) says build length varies and describes an aim to give customers a three-week start-to-finish window. Add a concise, sourced post-approval/build stage and a caveated timing answer, or narrow the eyebrow and hero promise if the page intentionally covers only the pre-commitment path. Evidence: [320 process](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/outputs/gardenroom-r5-visual-review/screenshots/320x568-process-section.png), [1440 full page](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/outputs/gardenroom-r5-visual-review/screenshots/1440x900-full.png).

### P2 - The price promise is named but its buyer benefit is omitted

[index.html:178](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/work/community-acceptance-20260920/runs/external-reference-r5/project/index.html:178) says the company publishes a like-for-like price promise and asks visitors to enquire about it. The [client contact page](https://gardenroomco.com/contact-us/) states the material conditional benefit: if a lower like-for-like garden-room quote is found, the company says it will beat it. The current wording is cautious and not false, but it withholds the strongest concrete answer in an otherwise generic quote-factors section. State that conditional promise accurately beside the comparison requirement, and point to the source/terms; do not add an unsourced price range. Evidence: [390 quote](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/outputs/gardenroom-r5-visual-review/screenshots/390x844-quote-section.png), [1440 quote](/Users/mac/Documents/Codex/2026-09-17/co/outputs/gardenroom-r5-visual-review/screenshots/1440x900-quote-section.png).
