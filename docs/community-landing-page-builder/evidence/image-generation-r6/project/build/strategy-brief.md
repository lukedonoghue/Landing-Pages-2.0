# Clarentis landing page strategy

Research date: 2026-09-20. Primary source: https://clarentis.co.uk/ (homepage HTML, CSS, logo and rendered browser capture). Built-in references: Blue Mountain could not be opened through the web research tool; its structural criteria were used from the attached skill. Clean Slate Land Solutions was inspected at https://www.cleanslatelandsolutions.com/ for execution quality only.

## Buyer and action

- Buyer: UK startup, sole trader or small limited company owner looking for practical bookkeeping, accounting or tax help. They likely know the task or deadline but may not know the right scope.
- Intended action: email Clarentis to request a fixed-fee quote and arrange a free initial consultation. A verified public telephone number is secondary.
- Offer: an initial consultation, followed by a scoped fixed-fee proposal before work starts. This is supported by the official page's onboarding and fee guide.
- Delivery mechanism: understand the business and current records, agree scope and fee, organise HMRC/software setup, then provide the agreed ongoing or filing support.
- Main objection: uncertainty about fee, coverage and what happens next. The page answers these with the complete published indicative guide and a plain process.
- Trust anchor: specific published services, fee ranges, fixed-fee proposal and free initial consultation. No client reviews, accreditation or team credentials were found on the supplied page, so none are asserted.
- Traffic intent: inferred search/direct interest in small-business accounting, tax and bookkeeping. No keyword or ad-account data was supplied.

## Source conversion contract

The source page is **email-led**. Its header consultation, hero fixed-fee quote, startup offer and contact links all use `mailto:info@clarentis.co.uk`. The source has no form, required fields, consent checkbox, backend success state or booking calendar. It asks visitors to state business type and support needs in an email. The page promises a free initial consultation and a clear fixed-fee proposal after a scope review. The local build retains a direct mailto action using the exact primary label `Request your fixed-fee quote`. It does not claim the email was sent or show a thank-you state. The email app and separate Send step are disclosed near the hero action. Public phone: `07467 474356`, source https://clarentis.co.uk/ footer; displayed near the top and at final contact. No named contact is used.

## Visual direction

- Three adjectives: **clear** (plain fee table and compact headings), **capable** (large service-specific imagery and precise descriptions), **approachable** (human-scale photograph and practical copy).
- Source palette from official CSS: navy `#08294a`, ink `#092946`, teal `#159aaa`, darker teal `#0b7185`, soft teal `#eaf7f8`, pale background `#f6f8fb`. The logo is navy and teal on white.
- Applied palette: navy `#092946` for text and final contact, darker navy `#082238` for the compact mobile hero, dark teal `#0b7185` for accessible filled actions, source soft teal for the information strip, white and very light neutral `#f5f8f7` for reading space. Bright teal is an accent only. The generated hero's olive wall is an illustrative photographic color, not a new brand color.
- Typography provenance: no formal brand guide found. Official CSS declares `Inter, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`; no Inter web font is loaded. `build/brand.json` records rendered heading and prose glyphs as `.SF NS` in Chrome on macOS. Applied CSS uses the same declared stack and therefore the same rendered system sans on the tested host. No substitution is claimed.
- Type hierarchy: large but bounded hero, section headings sized for reading, small labels used sparingly, no negative letter spacing. Stable fixed breakpoints, not viewport-width type scaling.
- Composition: full-width photographic hero with copy in its real left-side negative space at desktop; stacked copy and unobstructed photograph on mobile. Following sections alternate image-led service explanation, list, fee table, process and open FAQ. Square edges and fine rules replace the source's pill/card repetition.
- Image roles: four distinct generated illustrative images, never proof. Each receives visible disclosure and accurate alt text. The original official logo is used on white.
- Motion: native scroll only, disabled for reduced-motion preference. No entrance animations.
- Visual-age checkpoint: preserve logo, navy/teal and system-sans character; leave behind the source's oversized hero, stacked rounded cards and gradient/pill motif. The restrained table and editorial photographs suit an owner comparing fee and scope.
