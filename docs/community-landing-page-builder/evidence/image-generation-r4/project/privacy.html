Privacy on this page

The quote form prepares an email draft. This page does not send the draft or store its contents on a server.

When you send the email

Your email app handles sending. If you choose to send the draft, it goes to info@clarentis.co.uk with the information you entered. Clarentis can use those details to respond to your enquiry.

Cookies and analytics

This page does not set analytics or marketing cookies and does not send form entries to an analytics service.

Questions about your information

For questions about how Clarentis handles an enquiry after you send it, contact info@clarentis.co.uk or call 07467 474356.
