Good to know

Questions worth asking early.

Every garden and brief is different. These answers are a starting point for your conversation.

Will I need planning permission?

Qualifying outbuildings generally do not need planning permission, but the criteria depend on the building and site. The team can discuss your circumstances.

How long does a build take?

Every build is different. The company aims to give a three-week window from start to finish; the actual schedule is discussed for your project.

Does a garden room need foundations?

Yes. The right base depends on ground conditions, access, drainage, levels and building size.

Can the room be designed for more than one use?

Yes. The portfolio includes rooms combining work, guest space and storage. Tell the team everything you want the space to do.
