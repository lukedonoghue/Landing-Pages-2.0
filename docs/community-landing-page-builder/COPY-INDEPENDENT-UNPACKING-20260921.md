## Blue Mountain

**Observable.** The buyer wants clear gutters and protection from debris, pests, flooding, and bushfire risk. The sequence is: outcome and product choice; brochure/pricing transaction; reviews; why trust; mechanism; fit, appearance, and warranty; selection, measurement, and DIY or arranged installation; benefit recap and CTA. Each section mostly advances a different decision.

The provider-specific choice mechanism is steel or aluminium mesh, a Mesh Matcher, satellite roof measurement, colour matching, and two installation routes. Proof offered includes 25 years, 20,000+ customers, reviews, a 12-year warranty, and "up to 50% stronger" materials.

**Hypothesis and weaknesses.** Early proof plus a brochure/pricing exchange may reduce uncertainty in a comparison-heavy purchase. Explaining how rain passes while debris stays out plausibly strengthens the benefit. But "lasts a lifetime" conflicts with the 12-year warranty; "50% stronger" lacks a visible comparator or test; bundled safety claims lack limits; and reviews evidence service experience more than durability. Repetition, copy errors, and stacked superlatives weaken authority. The labelled BEFORE/AFTER block is not a real state contrast, illustrating how a preset component can distort an otherwise copy-first argument.

## Clean Slate

**Observable.** The buyer wants overgrown land made usable and fears guesswork, poor communication, and surprises. The page names services and counties; identifies Brad and Tony; offers a guide and free estimate; shows a 5.0 Google rating with 22 reviews; presents why-choose claims and a two-step form; describes service categories; then follows assessment through completed work.

Its credible potential advantage is an owner-visible, communication-led process: discuss the goal, inspect access, vegetation, terrain, drainage, and scope, then bring suitable equipment. "Professional crew," "on time," and "modern CAT equipment" remain parity claims until evidence makes them distinctive. Reviews support communication and satisfaction, not universal speed, price, or technical outcomes.

**Hypothesis and weaknesses.** Named people and a complete delivery path may reduce contractor-risk anxiety. The estimate transaction is clear: about a minute of input and follow-up within 24 hours. The brochure and Get/Request/Start My Free Estimate labels create different readiness paths; that may be useful, but the hierarchy should be deliberate. Cost, duration, clearing-versus-mulching selection, debris handling, insurance, and site constraints mostly live elsewhere. The calm local voice beats generic hype, though several trust claims still need proof.

## Copy-contract critique

The three community files materially improve the original `conversion-copywriter` skill. They put copy before layout, replace fixed section counts with buyer-decision jobs, retain useful technical vocabulary, require a source-to-benefit chain, distinguish category benefit from reason-to-choose, keep unknowns out of copy, and align page, form, success, and follow-up. `copy-workflow.md` adds freshness hashes and evidence-aware review.

The original still supplies good defaults: one audience and offer, adjacent proof, objection mapping, claim qualification, honest scarcity, and follow-up consistency. Its universals are unsupported as requirements: a number in every headline; no question headlines; a fixed 13-section ESCAPE order; testimonials always before the sell; mandatory red/green before-after cards; exactly three process steps; identical CTA wording everywhere; three friction reducers under every button; zero jargon; every sentence expressed as an outcome; and centered hero layout. These mix editorial, interface, and house-style preferences. They can cause invented numerics, repetition, lost mechanism detail, or layout-led copy. Unbounce calls common elements a [guide](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/the-anatomy-of-a-landing-page/); [NN/g research](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/applying-writing-guidelines-web-pages/) supports scannable, concise, objective writing, not those formulas. [NN/g also allows feature terms](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/user-centric-language/) when readers use them and benefits are explained.

## Missing requirements

- Classify each reason as table stakes, useful advantage, comparative advantage, or verified uniqueness.
- Grade proof: self-assertion, customer experience, demonstration, internal data, or independent evidence; state what each can support.
- Map every major benefit to mechanism, boundary, evidence, and objection.
- Define the CTA exchange: input, deliverable, channel, timing, follow-up, conditions, privacy, and failure path.
- Rank objections from buyer evidence; do not reward unprioritized coverage.
- Capture traffic, awareness, alternatives, and real-brand voice samples before drafting.
- Escalate regulated, safety, guarantee, financial, and comparative claims for specialist review.

## Low-cost pre-build acceptance

1. **Contract, 20 minutes:** freeze audience, traffic assumption, offer, CTA exchange, voice sample, top three objections, prohibited claims, and unknowns.
2. **Argument map, 25 minutes:** record each section's decision job and `claim -> mechanism -> evidence -> limit -> objection`. Remove duplication and narrow unsupported differentiation.
3. **Independent edit, 30 minutes:** read headings alone, then copy aloud. Check entailment, qualifiers, objective tone, transaction consistency, and the complete delivery path. Classify blocker, improvement, or polish; revise once.
4. **Comprehension check, under one hour:** show the first screen, then headings, to 3-5 target-like readers. Ask what is offered, for whom, why choose it, what proves it, what happens after the CTA, and what remains unclear. This diagnoses comprehension; it does not validate conversion.
5. **Acceptance:** every material or high-risk claim is traceable; no reader confidently misunderstands the transaction; four of five can state service, benefit, and next step; the top three objections have specific answers; and no section exists only to satisfy a template.

Layout starts after this gate. Rendered-copy checks remain separate, and only traffic data or a properly designed experiment can support conversion claims.
