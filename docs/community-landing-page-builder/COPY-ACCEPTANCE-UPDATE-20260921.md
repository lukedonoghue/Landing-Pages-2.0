## What The References Teach

| Reference observation | Why it may help | Applied instruction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue Mountain combines product selection, measurement, fit and installation routes | Gives buyers specific help choosing, not just a list of product benefits | Select a supported reason to choose before drafting; explain the capability that produces the benefit |
| Brochure/pricing offer appears early | Makes an enquiry a concrete exchange | State the actual deliverable and what happens next, consistently across page, form, guide and success message |

The useful transfer is an argument, not copied headings, a colour palette or a fixed section count. The page must connect: desired result, specific capability, credible evidence, important objections, clear next step.

## What Changed In The Community Fork

1. Research now separates ordinary expected service, useful advantage, supported comparison and proven uniqueness. It selects the strongest supported angle instead of collecting generic positives.
2. The brief records buyer benefit, mechanism, evidence, limits and the objection answered. Customer concerns are marked as observed or inferred; brand voice uses actual language samples when available.
3. Proof is scoped: a company assertion, customer experience, demonstration and independent evidence cannot automatically support the same claims.
4. Copy comes before layout. First write the complete argument, then revise for benefits and specificity, then perform the editorial acceptance pass. These are bounded passes, not an indefinite rewriting loop.
5. A reviewer first reconstructs offer, benefit, reason to choose and next step from the copy alone. Answers that exist only in the research brief expose missing copy.
6. The review must challenge the strongest reason not to accept the draft, inspect sources and give exact passages for its verdicts.
7. A local verifier blocks missing/stale review evidence and unresolved criteria. The existing structured copy audit invokes it for newly prepared contexts. Lightweight pages use the same gate without the full Worker drafting schema.
8. After layout, the accepted copy master remains authoritative. Rendered-copy/readability checks remain separate because a good draft does not prove faithful implementation.

Eight acceptance criteria: message match, claim support, outcome and mechanism, objection coverage, headline story, voice and density, offer consistency, reference adaptation.

## What Was Deliberately Not Added

No mandatory human reader panel, extra subscription, API key, new MCP server, universal numeric headline, compulsory fear/urgency, fixed emotional order or extra owner approval. Existing user-required CTA consistency and paid-page exit restrictions remain. Optional wording preferences do not trigger a new full rewrite. A separate reviewer is used when available and authorized; otherwise self-review must be disclosed.

The original skill's useful buyer motivation, adjacent proof, objection mapping, qualifications and follow-up consistency remain. Its rigid formulas were not imported wholesale.
