Privacy information

How this demonstration handles test data

This is not the real Clarentis website. It is a separate demonstration of a landing page, enquiry form and CRM. Do not submit real personal, customer, tax, payroll or financial information.

Who operates this demonstration

The operator contact for this demonstration must be supplied before public publication. Clarentis is not the controller of data entered into this separate demo.

What the form stores

After the CRM confirms a test enquiry, the isolated Cloudflare D1 database stores the synthetic fields entered in the form, the selected service and contact preference, a receipt identifier, timestamps and the record's CRM stage. The site does not send an email or webhook to Clarentis.

Optional measurement and campaign details

Optional page measurement and campaign attribution are off until you choose to allow them. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals take priority. You can reopen Privacy choices from the footer and withdraw optional collection at any time.

When advertising matching is configured and separately allowed, the browser normalises and irreversibly hashes the submitted email and international-format phone number before placing them in the advertising data event. Raw contact details are not placed in analytics or the data layer.

Why the data is used

Synthetic submissions are used only to demonstrate receipt creation, CRM storage, pipeline management, notes, reporting and consent-aware conversion tracking. This build does not configure marketing email, outbound calls or third-party lead webhooks.

Service provider and storage

The landing page, Worker, authenticated CRM and database are designed to run in one user-selected Cloudflare account. Cloudflare provides the hosting, security and database infrastructure. Production account details will be recorded after the owner authorises the intended destination.

Retention and removal

The demonstration will be configured to retain synthetic enquiries for no more than 30 days before public handoff. The CRM owner can also hide a test contact or permanently erase its enquiry details, notes, activity and delivery records. Changing Privacy choices does not erase a stored enquiry.

Your choices

You may decline optional data and still submit a synthetic test enquiry. Do not use this demonstration for a real enquiry. To contact the real business, visit clarentis.co.uk or call 07467 474356.

Contact

A working demonstration-operator contact address is required before publication. It will be used for privacy questions and requests relating to test records in this isolated CRM.

Demonstration only. No real personal data should be submitted.
