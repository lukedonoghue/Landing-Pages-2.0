Privacy information

How this demonstration handles test data

This is not the real Clarentis website. It is a separate demonstration of a landing page, enquiry form and CRM. Do not submit real personal, customer, tax, payroll or financial information.

Who operates this demonstration

Demo operator identity: Test demo operator. Test-only contact label: test@example.invalid, which is deliberately non-deliverable. Clarentis is not the controller of data entered into this separate demo.

What the form stores

After the CRM confirms a test enquiry, the isolated Cloudflare D1 database stores the synthetic fields entered in the form, the selected service and contact preference, a receipt identifier, timestamps and the record's CRM stage. The site does not send an email or webhook to Clarentis.

Campaign details and measurement

Campaign parameters and an external referrer may be stored with a submitted synthetic enquiry so the CRM can show where that enquiry came from. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals prevent this campaign capture.

Visitor analytics and advertising matching are disabled on this demonstration. It does not load Google Tag Manager or place submitted contact details in an advertising data event.

Why the data is used

Synthetic submissions are used only to demonstrate receipt creation, CRM storage, campaign attribution, pipeline management, notes and reporting. This build does not configure visitor analytics, advertising matching, marketing email, outbound calls or third-party lead webhooks.

Service provider and storage

The landing page, Worker, authenticated CRM and database are designed to run in one user-selected Cloudflare account. Cloudflare provides the hosting, security and database infrastructure. Production account details will be recorded after the owner authorises the intended destination.

Retention and removal

The demonstration will be configured to retain synthetic enquiries for no more than 30 days before public handoff. The CRM owner can also hide a test contact or permanently erase its enquiry details, notes, activity and delivery records.

Test-only use

Do not use this demonstration for a real enquiry. To contact the real business, visit clarentis.co.uk or call 07467 474356.

Contact

This isolated demo uses test@example.invalid as a non-deliverable test-only contact label. It does not provide a real rights-request channel, so do not enter real personal data. The Cloudflare account owner can manage or erase the clearly synthetic records created during verification.

Demonstration only. No real personal data should be submitted.
