Before you enquire

Straight answers to practical questions

The free consultation is the place to confirm the exact scope. These answers cover the published starting point.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Clarentis publishes a free initial consultation. It is used to understand your business, deadlines, current records and the support you need before a proposal is prepared.

Are the published prices guaranteed?

No. They are indicative ranges. The final fixed fee depends on the agreed scope, transaction volume and complexity.

Can I ask about more than one service?

Yes. Choose the closest service in the form and describe the wider picture in your message. The scope review can cover connected bookkeeping, payroll, VAT and tax work.

Do my books need to be up to date before I enquire?

No. Explain what is in place and what feels overdue or unclear. The consultation is designed to establish the current position and the next practical step.
