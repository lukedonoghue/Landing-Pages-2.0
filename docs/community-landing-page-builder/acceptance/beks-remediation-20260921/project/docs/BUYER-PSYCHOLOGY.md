# Buyer Psychology

## Audience

Small-business owners using or considering QuickBooks Online who need recurring bookkeeping, a cleanup/catch-up project, A/P or A/R support, or clearer KPI context. This is a demand-side hypothesis derived from the published service mix, not a claim about actual customers.

## Desired after-state

- Know which bookkeeping starting point best matches the current problem.
- Understand that dashboard or advisory work is separately scoped rather than automatically included.
- Enter an initial conversation with records, reconciliation questions, and priorities organized.

## Internal monologue

- "I know the books need attention, but I do not know whether this is monthly work or a cleanup."
- "I want more than tidy transactions; I need to know what deserves attention."
- "Will this work with QuickBooks Online and my existing workflow?"
- "Can I learn enough about fit before committing to an unknown price?"

## Buying criteria

- Clear service fit and scope boundaries.
- Familiarity with QuickBooks Online.
- A practical path from bookkeeping maintenance to useful management context.
- Geographic or remote-service fit.
- A low-friction, truthful first step with no invented urgency or guaranteed result.
