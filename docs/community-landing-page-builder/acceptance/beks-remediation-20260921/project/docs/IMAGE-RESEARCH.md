# Image Research

## Search queries and sources

- Audited the official homepage and its rendered image inventory.
- Inspected only the single official portrait for identity and role; no broad stock search was needed after generation was authorized.
- Full provenance, prompts, hashes, variants, attempt accounting, and review reports are in `build/image-plan.json` and `research/image-evidence/`.

## Candidate inventory

- Official portrait: research-only, not published because republication permission was not established.
- Generated hero workspace: illustrative, no person, logos, readable records, or real customer data.
- Generated cleanup scene: anonymous hands and generic paperwork, explicitly non-documentary.
- Generated dashboard conversation: anonymous figures and unlabeled charts, with no real metrics or results.
- Checklist cover: derived from the actual project-authored PDF page 1.

## Identity and provenance checks

- No generated person is presented as Beks, an employee, or a customer.
- All three generated assets are classified as illustrative and disclosed in the page captions.
- Native image generation did not report an exact model; the allowed unverified-model exception and tool evidence are recorded.
- The three initial unrecorded attempts are marked failed. The three selected attempts preserve exact prompts and output hashes.

## Placement coverage

- Hero: full-bleed bookkeeping workspace with copy-safe area.
- Service fit: 4:3 cleanup/organization scene.
- Dashboard context: 3:2 planning scene.
- Guide: actual cover preview with direct PDF link.
- Responsive WebP derivatives are used at 480, 960, and source-appropriate larger widths.

## Generation decision and gaps

Generation was necessary because the source supplied one portrait with unresolved reuse rights and no service-explainer imagery. The generated scenes fill the hero, cleanup, and advisory-context roles without serving as proof.

## Rejected candidates

- Official portrait: not licensed for republication in this demonstration.
- First three generation outputs: rejected from final use because exact prompts were not persisted at generation time.
- Any image implying a real client file, testimonial, staff identity, certification, financial result, or before/after outcome.
