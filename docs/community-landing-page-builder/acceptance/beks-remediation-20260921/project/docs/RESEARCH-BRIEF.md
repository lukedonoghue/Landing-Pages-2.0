## Client facts

- Published services include monthly bookkeeping, cleanup/catch-up, accounts payable/receivable, and consulting.
- QuickBooks Online is the preferred accounting platform.
- The official site describes service in Kansas, Missouri, and remotely.
- A cleanup/catch-up discount may be available with a monthly service contract; no amount or eligibility rule is published.
- The Chamber directory describes the business as a QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor, Strategic Advisor, and Custom KPI Dashboard Builder.
- The official homepage supplied one portrait. Republication rights were not established, so the portrait is research-only and is not shipped.
- No verified testimonials, ratings, customer counts, response times, prices, awards, or guaranteed outcomes were found.

## Reference-page findings

- The source site leads with a free-consultation action and a portrait-led first screen.
- The strongest supportable differentiation is the connected service mix: QuickBooks Online bookkeeping with separately scoped KPI-dashboard and strategic-advisory context.
- The demo preserves the free-consultation request but routes it only to an isolated synthetic CRM. It does not book, email, call, or submit to the real business.
- Missing social proof is replaced with sourced service facts and a useful public month-end checklist, not fabricated customer evidence.

## Open questions

- Exact engagement scope, pricing, response times, and dashboard inclusion require a real consultation and are not promised here.
- Custom publication on `go.netbean.com` and `crm.netbean.com` requires the `netbean.com` zone in the connected Cloudflare account or owner-approved DNS setup.
- GTM, Google Ads, consent-provider, and sender/recipient verification values are absent; those integrations remain disabled.
