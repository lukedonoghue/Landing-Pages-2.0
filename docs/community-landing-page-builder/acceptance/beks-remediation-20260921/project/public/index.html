Before you begin

Questions to settle before a bookkeeping conversation.

Do I need ongoing bookkeeping or a cleanup project?

Monthly bookkeeping fits records that need recurring attention. Cleanup or catch-up fits a backlog, reconciliation issue, or prior period that needs review. The consultation is where the likely starting point can be confirmed.

Is there a cleanup or catch-up discount?

A discount may be available when cleanup or catch-up is paired with a monthly service contract. Ask during the consultation about eligibility, amount, and pricing.

Does Bookkeeping by Beks work only in Lawrence?

No. Service is offered in Kansas, Missouri, and remotely, subject to confirmation for the individual business.

How do KPI dashboards and consulting fit?

A custom KPI dashboard can organize selected measures for regular review, and consulting can help frame questions around them. Use the consultation to confirm metrics, availability, scope, and pricing.
