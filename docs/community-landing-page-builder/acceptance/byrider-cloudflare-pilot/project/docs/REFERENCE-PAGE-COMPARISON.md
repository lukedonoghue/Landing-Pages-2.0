# Reference Page Comparison

| Position | Visible pattern | Persuasive job | Evidence used | Objection handled | CTA behavior | Byrider adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outcome-led split hero with identity, proof bullets, and a substantial media/guide panel | Establish outcome and trust before asking for action | Client service imagery and owner video | “Who is behind this?” | Primary estimate action plus phone | Integrated-model promise, official dealership imagery, guide preview, no unsupported revenue headline |
| 2 | Guide appears as a real object near the top | Make the offer concrete | Rendered brochure cover | “What will I receive?” | Download/request action | Gated Franchise Guide with model, support, investment questions, and diligence path |
| 3 | Customer proof follows the offer | Reduce risk early | Verified public review | “Can they deliver?” | Continue to estimate | Attributed franchise-owner statement from Byrider's first-party site |
| 4 | Capability cards translate inputs into experience | Explain why the provider is credible | Equipment, experience, communication | “Why this company?” | Mid-page CTA | Training, technology, underwriting, marketing, development, and operator-led ownership |
| 5 | Service cards match situations to solutions | Help buyers self-select | Service scope | “Is this for my project?” | Repeated primary action | Dealer principal, finance leader, multi-unit owner, and business-leader fit paths |
| 6 | Short process reduces uncertainty | Show what happens next | Actual operating process | “What am I committing to?” | Primary CTA | Guide review, discovery conversation, FDD/advisor diligence; opening path explained separately |
| 7 | FAQ precedes a strong final action | Resolve final objections | First-party facts | Price, timing, fit | Same CTA | Qualified investment, training, financing, earnings/FDD, timeline, and next-step answers |

The benchmark's media cadence is retained through a rich hero, early guide artifact, proof portrait, model diagram, dealership and training imagery, technology mockup, process visual, and closing guide image. Its colors, wording, geometry, client proof, and local-service identity are not copied.
