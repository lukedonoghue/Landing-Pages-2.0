## Section: faq

### Straight Answers for a High-Consideration Decision

Use these answers as a starting point, then verify current terms in Byrider’s Franchise Disclosure Document.

### Do I need automotive experience?

No specific automotive background is required. Byrider describes qualified candidates from automotive and other professional backgrounds, but expects strong leadership, financial management, team development, sufficient capitalization, and disciplined execution. Training covers sales, service, finance, accounting, compliance, management, and the integrated technology platform.

### What are the current franchise and royalty fees?

Byrider currently lists a $60,000 initial franchise fee for the first location and $35,000 for each additional location purchased. Its FAQ lists ongoing royalties of 2.5% of gross vehicle sales and 1% of collections. Review the current FDD for complete terms, caps, assumptions, and other obligations.

### Does Byrider finance the franchise investment?

Byrider says it does not directly provide or guarantee franchisee financing. It may facilitate introductions to financial institutions familiar with the model and automotive receivables. Every financing decision remains subject to the lender’s requirements and approval.

### How much can a Byrider franchise make?

Byrider does not make revenue, profit, or financial performance projections outside Item 19 of the current FDD. Actual results vary based on market, competition, capitalization, costs, management, and execution. Review Item 19 and conduct your own independent evaluation.

### How long does opening take?

Byrider says a new store opens in nine to 12 months on average. Site development, financing, facility requirements, and other project factors can change the timeline.
