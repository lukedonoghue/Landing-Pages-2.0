# Research Brief

Retrieved 18 September 2026 from Byrider's current first-party franchise pages, the existing `go.` funnel, official LinkedIn/editorial leads, and the supplied Clean Slate benchmark. Raw HTML, normalized text, brand measurements, and desktop/mobile captures are retained in `research/` and `build/`.

## Business and audience

Byrider Franchising Partners, LLC offers a vertically integrated used-vehicle dealership and finance franchise that combines vehicle sales, financing, and service. The page is for experienced operators, dealer principals, multi-unit owners, and business leaders who need to understand operating fit, capitalization, support, and the formal evaluation process before advancing.

## Offer and conversion

- Primary offer: Byrider Franchise Guide.
- Exact CTA: **Get the Franchise Guide**.
- Delivery: immediate download on the thank-you page; the guide is not the FDD.
- Follow-up: **A Byrider Franchise Development representative will follow up using your preferred contact method.**
- The later formal decision path should direct prospects to Byrider's current FDD and their own advisors.

## Sourced differentiators

- One integrated model spans used-vehicle sales, financing, and service.
- Byrider supports market evaluation, site selection, facility planning, opening preparation, training, marketing, technology, finance, and operations.
- Discover is the proprietary integrated dealership platform for sales, financing, collections, customer communication, reporting, and compliance.
- The current franchise fee and ongoing royalties are published on the official investment/FAQ pages, with complete terms deferred to the current FDD.
- The franchisor announced in 2024 that a group of Byrider franchisees acquired its franchising assets, providing a credible operator-led ownership story.
