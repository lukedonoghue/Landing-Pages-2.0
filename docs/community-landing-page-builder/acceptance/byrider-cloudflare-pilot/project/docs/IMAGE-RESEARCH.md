## Placement coverage and gap decision

- Hero/final CTA: official dealership exterior.
- Offer: lightweight cover image rendered from the approved brochure PDF.
- Early proof: official Dale Boone portrait.
- Operator proof: official acquisition group photograph.
- Investment/process: current official graphics, surrounded by qualifiers from the canonical copy.
- Technology: one generated illustrative workspace because first-party technology sources provide only a low-resolution duplicate dealership crop and generic stock imagery.

The generated image must show a plausible integrated dealership operations workspace with vehicle, finance, service, and reporting cues, no brand marks, no readable figures, no named product interface, and no people presented as Byrider staff or franchisees. Adjacent disclosure: **Illustrative image. Not an actual franchisee, employee, customer, or location.**
