# Clean Slate Case Study

Use this only to understand decisions and failure recovery. Do not reuse client-specific content.

## Successful adaptation

- Reference: Blue Mountain gutter-mesh landing page.
- Client: Central Florida land-clearing company.
- Adapted jobs: prominent phone, outcome hero, brochure/estimate offer, early reviews, method-fit explanation, proof strip, simple process, FAQ, repeated CTA.
- Brand adaptation: navy/gold palette, CAT-equipment imagery, local service language.
- Offer: downloadable services catalogue plus free custom site estimate.
- Funnel: modal-only four-step form, thank-you page, brochure download.

## Iterations that materially improved the result

- Replaced dense company-led paragraphs with the buyer's site-ready after-state.
- Distinguished full clearing from forestry mulching to prevent the wrong-method objection.
- Restored hero image visibility after a large white CTA card obscured the equipment.
- Moved a compact brochure CTA onto the blue hero and placed testimonial proof immediately beneath it.
- Replaced duplicated header CTA with a prominent click-to-call phone number.
- Removed unrequested photo upload and open-ended property-description fields.
- Verified public reviews against the client's website and exact Google listing; used public avatars only when tied to the same reviewer.
- Converted large raster assets to responsive WebP variants and self-hosted a small font set.

## Failures to prevent

- Inline form plus modal form when the user requested modal-only.
- Plausible but unrequested qualification fields.
- CTA wording drifting between page, form, and thank-you.
- Full-page screenshots showing blank lazy images because the test never scrolled.
- A local thank-you redirect being mistaken for delivered lead data.
- QA state saying `pass` while required screenshots or Escape behavior are still missing.
- Stub research artifacts contradicting later live-scrape logs.

## Publishing lesson

The final client archive contained static page files, all local assets, the website brochure, a separate brochure copy, and `START-HERE.txt`. Local asset and ZIP integrity passed. Lead delivery and advertising analytics remained pending, so the correct description was “locally verified publishing package,” not “live” or “production-ready.”
